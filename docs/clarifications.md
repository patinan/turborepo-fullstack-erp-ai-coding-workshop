# Clarification register

The clarification register required by TOR §10.1 for the Retail ERP release 1. It records every question the TOR leaves open and every rule the supplier has added or interpreted, with the decision, who approves it, and which requirements and tests depend on it.

Rules for this register:

- **Status** is one of `TOR baseline` (the TOR already states the release 1 position), `Proposed (supplier) — pending RetailOne`, `Approved`, `Rejected`, `Deferred`, or `Under consideration` (change requests).
- A requirement that depends on an entry not yet `Approved` must not be used for acceptance (TOR §7.3). The traceability matrix (`docs/traceability.md`) marks those rows.
- Approval is recorded by filling in **Approved by** and **Date approved**; a discussion or demo does not count (TOR §10.2).
- Supplier-raised items use IDs `C-01`, `C-02`, …; TOR questions keep their `Q-` IDs; change requests keep their `CR-` IDs.
- "Tests" refers to acceptance criterion IDs in `docs/specs/release-1.md`.

Proposed date for all supplier positions below: **2026-10-26**. Approving stakeholder is the **RetailOne business owner** unless stated otherwise.
